# Prompt — External Candidate Orbit (Outside the Profile Card)

Copy/paste this prompt for future animation work:

---

ROLE: Principal UI/UX Motion Designer + Frontend Animation Engineer.

PROJECT: TalentBridgeAI homepage candidate showcase.

TASK: Candidate portraits must orbit **OUTSIDE** the main profile card.

STRICT RULES:
1. Orbit path = elliptical track around the **exterior perimeter** of the main white profile card.
2. DO NOT place orbiting avatars inside the card.
3. DO NOT place them over name, skills, experience, education, or match score.
4. DO NOT rotate portraits inside the rectangular photo frame.
5. Next candidate: highlight on the outer orbit → travel from outside into the centre portrait → update details together → cinematic zoom on the portrait only.
6. Keep continuous slow orbit (about 12–18s per loop).
7. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
8. Do not redesign unrelated homepage sections.

VERIFY IN BROWSER:
- Avatars clearly move outside the card.
- At least 3 full selection cycles.
- No wrong image/name pairing.

---

**Hindi short version:**  
Candidate ki chhoti photos **card ke bahar** round karein, andar nahi. Jab turn aaye tab bahar se centre portrait me aaye, phir zoom ho.
