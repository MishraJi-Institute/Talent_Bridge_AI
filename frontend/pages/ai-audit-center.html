AI TRANSPARENCY

Understand the role of AI in this experience.

Clear boundaries matter. This page explains which AI features are and are not connected in the current Django application.

!
No AI provider is connectedThis application does not run AI-generated resume writing, candidate scoring, hiring decisions, or model-based job matching. No model or independent audit reports are available.

What works today?

Job Search filters eligible Django database listings. Recommended Jobs ranks eligible roles using profile skills and location, without an AI model.

Browse jobs

What is not active?

AI-written resumes, automated candidate recommendations, resume scoring, candidate ranking, and AI-based hiring decisions are not connected.
