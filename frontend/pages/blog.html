Practical ideas for your next career move.

Thoughtful, useful starting points for candidates and hiring teams—focused on clarity, preparation, and fairer processes.

Job seekers

Make your skills visible in a resume

Use specific examples to show what you did, the tools you used, and the result. Keep the language close to the skills and responsibilities in the role description, without overstating your experience.

Review resume guidance
Hiring teams

Write a job post candidates can evaluate

Describe the work, required skills, location, employment type, and compensation range where possible. Separate essential qualifications from preferences so candidates can make an informed decision.

Responsible technology

Keep human judgment in the hiring loop

Use clear criteria, check information in context, and give people a way to ask questions. Automated tools should not replace thoughtful review or communication.
