TALENTBRIDGE HELP

Answers to common questions.

Practical guidance about job search, accounts, applications, and employer workflows on this Django-backed platform.

Job seekers

How do I find a role?

Search listings by title, skill, company, or location. Demo roles are labeled; approved employers' live listings are kept separate from samples.

Browse jobs
Applications

Did my application reach an employer?

A submitted application to a real listing is saved to your account and appears in your Applications page. Demo-role previews do not submit an application.

View your applications
Accounts

How do I reset my password?

Use the reset flow with the email address on your account. In this development setup, reset email is written to the configured local email backend rather than delivered externally.

Request password recovery
Employers

Why might employer approval be required?

New employer companies are reviewed before publishing access. Approved employers can manage their company and job workflows from the account dashboard.

Read employer approval guidance
Profiles

Can I save profile changes here?

Signed-in job seekers can edit their account-backed profile. Profile discovery is private by default and can be enabled in Privacy Settings.

Edit your profile
Privacy

Is AI matching active?

AI-generated matching and ranking are not connected. Recommended Jobs uses profile skills and location to rank eligible listings; it is not an AI decision.

Read AI transparency information

Still need help?

The contact form records a support inquiry for the team. Email delivery is not configured, so no email confirmation is sent.
