1 · Find the message

Check your inbox

If an account matches the address, Django sends a password reset link to the registered email. In local development, the message appears in the server terminal unless SMTP is configured.

2 · Choose a password

Use the secure link

Open the link on a trusted device. Recovery links can expire, so request another one if yours is no longer valid.

3 · Sign back in

Protect your account

After resetting, sign in through Django. Choose a unique password and keep it private. Do not forward the recovery email or share its link.
