CAREER GUIDE

Make remote work work for you.

Use this checklist to assess a role, prepare an application, and protect your time and information.

Browse remote jobs Estimate gross pay
Before you apply

Confirm what “remote” means

Check whether the position is remote within a specific country or region, whether occasional office visits are expected, and which time zones the team supports.

Review the employment type, working hours, compensation currency, benefits, and any equipment or connectivity requirements.

Application prep

Show how you collaborate

Use concise examples of written communication, independent planning, and sharing progress across a team. Describe the result and your contribution.

Tailor your resume to the role and keep personal information relevant. Do not send identity or banking documents before verifying a legitimate hiring process.

Stay safe

Watch for hiring scams

Verify the company and interviewer through independently found contact details. Be cautious of upfront fees, pressure to buy equipment from a named vendor, or requests to move money.

Use secure devices and networks for interviews, and do not share passwords, one-time codes, or account recovery links with recruiters.

Plan your setup

Make a sustainable routine

Ask how the team handles meetings, focus time, accessibility, onboarding, and time off. A clear schedule and a comfortable workspace help set realistic expectations.

Confirm any required travel, work authorization, and local employment terms before accepting an offer.
