FAQ

Common questions

Short answers for hiring teams and candidates.

What can I do on TalentBridge AI?
Employers post jobs and review applicants. Job seekers create a Smart Card, browse roles, and apply. Matching ranks fit from skills, experience, and preferences.
Is it free to post a job?
Starter accounts can post jobs and manage applicants. See Pricing for paid plan differences.
Where do candidates come from?
Candidates create profiles on TalentBridge and apply to published roles. Only approved employer listings are public.
How does matching work?
Fit scores compare role requirements with a candidate’s skills, experience, location, and work mode. Demo scores in this backup are illustrative.
How do I get started?
Create an account, choose employer or job seeker, then post a role or complete your Smart Card.
